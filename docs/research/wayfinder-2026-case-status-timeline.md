# Reconstructing 2026 candidacy status at the election — TSE case and result sources

**Research date:** 2026-10-13.  
**Question:** Can each 2026 federal-deputy `SQ_CANDIDATO` be associated with the decisions and appeals legally in force on 2026-10-04, distinguishing sub judice, final grant/refusal, and final vote destination?  
**Scope:** Official TSE/Justice Electoral systems, datasets, and law only. No candidate examples or personal details are reproduced.

## Finding

**Not as a complete, mechanically reproducible candidate-level snapshot from the public flat files alone.** There are public sources for (a) the candidacy roster/status view, (b) public court dockets and signed decisions, and (c) the election's totalization and vote-destination result. These make a documented, case-by-case reconstruction feasible where the relevant process and procedural history are found. But the public materials reviewed do not provide a single dated, versioned crosswalk `SQ_CANDIDATO → all registration/appeal process numbers → operative decision and effective date → final destination of votes`, nor an immutable 2026-10-04 extract of the status/result views. A current status value or later result cannot be treated as the 4 October state.

**Status for the ticket: leave open.** The sources establish the systems, legal categories and a viable manual evidence path, but do not prove complete nationwide coverage of candidacies and every related appeal or supply a dated, exhaustive candidate-to-case crosswalk. Exact gap and what would close it are listed below.

The public data portal identifies `SQ_CANDIDATO` as part of the candidate-data model in TSE documentation used by the project's other research. The PJe service, in contrast, indexes court processes by docket/process number. Thus a reconstruction must explicitly preserve both identifiers and document the crosswalk, rather than treating either candidate name or a current situation label as a durable join key. The resources inspected do not document a public, complete crosswalk from every `SQ_CANDIDATO` to every related docket and appeal.

## Legal distinction: case status is not result destination

1. **Sub judice is procedural and time-dependent.** Article 16-A of [Law 9,504/1997 (Elections Law)](https://www.tse.jus.br/legislacao/codigo-eleitoral/lei-das-eleicoes/lei-das-eleicoes-lei-nb0-9.504-de-30-de-setembro-de-1997) provides that a candidate whose registration is *sub judice* may campaign and have votes recorded subject to the law's treatment pending the final decision. TSE's [2026 explainer on sub judice candidacies](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Setembro/candidatura-sub-judice-entenda-como-funciona) states that votes for a registration denied while under appeal are counted as “anulados sub judice.” A denial under appeal therefore is not final refusal.
2. **Final grant/refusal must be established from procedural record, not just status field.** For each relevant case, collect the operative decision, any appeal and appellate decisions, notices/publication or other legally relevant event, and evidence that no appeal remained pending / the decision became final (where available). Record event/document date and time separately from a data-file generation date. Later events must not be applied retroactively without a legal basis.
3. **Vote destination is a separate election-administration fact.** [Resolution TSE 23,748/2026](https://www.tse.jus.br/legislacao/compilada/res/2026/resolucao-no-23-748-de-26-de-fevereiro-de-2026) governs proportional/majoritarian systems, vote destination, proclamation, and diploma rules. The TSE's [2026 destination-of-votes guide](https://www.tse.jus.br/eleicoes/arquivos/tabela-de-destinacao-de-votos-1/@@display-file/file/destinacao_de_votos_2026_web_final.pdf) and state totalization reports are the right sources for the destination that the election system applied. Do not collapse “valid,” “annulled sub judice,” “annulled,” and party/legend destination into one candidate status.
4. **Relevant calendar context.** [Resolution TSE 23,760/2026 (calendar)](https://www.tse.jus.br/legislacao/compilada/res/2026/resolucao-no-23-760-de-2-de-marco-de-2026) fixes the 2026 election calendar; TSE's official [candidate-registration deadline notice](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Agosto/prazo-para-registro-de-candidaturas-nas-eleicoes-2026-termina-neste-sabado-15) explains the filing window and 15 August filing deadline. This does not establish case-by-case finality by 4 October.

## Reproducible candidate-level protocol (where evidence exists)

1. Freeze and hash the official 2026 candidate CSV and candidate-history ZIP. Retain the source URLs, download time, internal generated/modified values, README/layout, and the exact rows for federal-deputy candidates. Key the roster on `(ANO_ELEICAO, SQ_CANDIDATO)` and retain state/cargo/registration attributes needed to audit identity. Never use name as the sole join.
2. For each candidate, capture any official registration process number shown in candidate detail/data. Query the PJe public interface by that number, verify the case class, election, office and party/candidate against the docket, then follow every linked appeal, appeal-internal incident, substitution/cancellation, and later related process. Where there is no explicit linkage, record the search method and avoid asserting exhaustive linkage from a name-only search.
3. Build an event ledger keyed by `(process number, document/event ID)`: court, event type, signed document/event timestamp, publication/notice timestamp where present, operative scope, appeal filed/decided, and evidence of finality. Read the decision text. Derive the status on 2026-10-04 only from events and legal rules whose temporal effect can be shown on or before that cutoff. Keep “unknown / not established” distinct from granted, refused, and pending.
4. Separately capture a state-level official totalization report/result vintage with its own generated timestamp and the candidate's vote-destination category. Label it as the report's state, not as the legal status on election day. If there are later revised reports, retain them separately; do not substitute a final later destination for an election-day snapshot.
5. Publish only candidate keys, procedural classification and source references necessary for the study. Avoid copying personal identifiers or sensitive allegations from pleadings.

## Timestamp evidence and exact unresolved gap

- DCC publicly reports an update time and an hourly cadence; this timestamps the portal refresh observed, not each candidate's operative judicial status.
- CKAN timestamps the publication/metadata records (candidate resource 22 July; candidacy history 16 September), while explicitly deferring the data's update date to inside the files. Those are not adequate alone to freeze the 04/10 cutoff.
- PJe case documents can establish dated judicial events. For any candidate-level timeline, document date, signature and relevant publication/notice or finality evidence are necessary; a current DCC/SIG status does not supply these.
- Totalization PDFs report a result vintage. The inspected official result report is timestamped 06/10/2026, so it documents a post-election state, not 04/10.

**Evidence gap preventing a full yes:** the reviewed official publications do not document an exhaustive join from every deputy-federal `SQ_CANDIDATO` to all its PJe registration and appellate dockets, nor provide a retained 04/10/2026 candidate-status/result snapshot with per-record effective timestamps. PJe also was not queryable automatically in this review because its public interface served a human-verification screen. Therefore this research does not claim the candidate-by-candidate universe is complete or resolve any individual status. Closure requires (a) the official candidate/history layout and event-time semantics, (b) reproducibly captured candidate-to-process crosswalk and complete related docket chains for the population, (c) dated evidence establishing operative/final status at the cutoff, and (d) the matching official initial totalization/destination vintage. A response to the TSE requesting confirmation of the crosswalk, status-history semantics and retained as-of result vintages would close the documentary gap if no public equivalent exists.
